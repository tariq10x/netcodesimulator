# Code of Conduct

NetcodeSim is intended to be useful to students, researchers, educators, and developers. Project discussions should stay respectful, technical, and welcoming.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful of differing experience levels and backgrounds.
- Keep feedback specific, constructive, and focused on the work.
- Assume good faith when asking for clarification.
- Credit ideas, reports, and contributions where appropriate.
- Help keep issues and pull requests actionable.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or repeated dismissive behavior.
- Publishing private contact details or other personal information without permission.
- Spam, trolling, or intentionally derailing project discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests; or block participation when behavior harms the project community.

If you need to report a conduct issue, contact the repository maintainer through the private contact method listed on the public repository.
